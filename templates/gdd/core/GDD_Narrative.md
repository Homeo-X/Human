# Narrative

## Premise & Themes
_The story in one paragraph; 1–3 themes and how MECHANICS express them
(ludonarrative alignment: if the story says scarcity, the economy should
feel scarce)._

## Characters
| Character | Role in Story | Role in Gameplay | Arc (one line) |
|---|---|---|---|

## Structure & Delivery
- Structure: linear / branching (map the branches!) / environmental
- Delivery methods ranked by intrusiveness (environmental → ambient VO →
  dialogue → cutscene) with a budget per method (→ Content scope table)
- Skippability policy: everything skippable; state what persists (subtitles
  log/codex) so skipping never destroys information

## Branching (if any)
| Decision Point | Options | What Actually Differs | Reconverges? |
|---|---|---|---|
_"What actually differs" is honest: flavor-only branches are fine but must
be known to be flavor-only._

## Localization & VO Notes
Word-count budget (drives loc cost), VO vs text-only decision, name/pun
constraints for translatability (→ i18n checklist decision).

## Acceptance Gates
_Narrative can't be Given/When/Then; it is gated: script review sign-off
(who), table-read/VO review, in-context review of every delivery moment on
the golden path. Gates registered in Acceptance_Criteria_and_DoD._
