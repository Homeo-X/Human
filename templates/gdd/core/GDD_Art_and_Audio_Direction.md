# Art & Audio Direction

## Visual Direction
- Style statement (one paragraph) + 3–5 reference images/boards named
- Readability rule: gameplay-critical elements always read against any
  background — palette/silhouette/outline strategy stated
- Technical style constraints: poly/texture budgets derive from the frame
  budget table in TECH_Game_Architecture; art that can't ship at frame
  rate isn't the art direction

## Audio Direction
- Tone statement; music approach (adaptive layers? stingers?); the 3
  sounds that must be iconic (core verb, reward, danger)
- Mix priorities: what ducks for what; dialogue always intelligible
- Silence policy: when the game deliberately goes quiet

## UI Art & Typography
Type choices legible at platform viewing distance (→ FR-PXP-003);
iconography language; motion/juice guidelines consistent with feel targets.
