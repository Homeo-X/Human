# Research Charter

## Research Question
_The question, one sentence, answerable in principle by the methods below.
Then: why it matters and to whom (the field, a decision, a system design)._

## Background & Prior Work
_What is already known, with the 3–5 works this study stands on — and the
specific gap none of them closes. A study without a named gap is a rerun._

## Hypothesis Register
_Every hypothesis falsifiable — the "What Would Kill It" column is the
falsifiability test (mirrors the pillar rule: a hypothesis nothing could
kill is a belief). Exploratory questions are allowed but LABELED
exploratory; they generate hypotheses, they don't confirm them._
| ID | Hypothesis | Type (confirmatory/exploratory) | What Would Kill It | Tested By (EXP refs) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HYP-1 | | | | |

## Constructs & Definitions
_Every load-bearing term operationalized: "emergence", "robustness",
"performance" mean nothing until measured. One row per construct → its
measurable proxy → the proxy's known limits._
| Construct | Operationalized As | Proxy Limits |
|---|---|---|

## Success Criteria for the STUDY (not the hypotheses)
| KPI id | Outcome | Measured By |
|---|---|---|
| G-01 | every confirmatory HYP reaches a pre-registered conclusion | Analysis_Plan criteria all dispositioned |
| G-02 | reproduction from clean checkout | Reproducibility gate |

## Non-Goals
_Questions this study deliberately does not answer — the scope fence
against post-hoc mission creep._

## Contribution Statement
_One paragraph, written NOW: what the field/decision gains if HYPs are
confirmed — and what it gains if they are refuted. If the refuted branch
is empty, the study is confirmation-shopping; redesign it._
